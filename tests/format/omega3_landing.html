How to take it:

  • Daily: 2 softgels once a day with a meal, swallowed whole with water. Do not exceed the stated serving.
  • Loading: none. No loading phase is established (Calder, 2018; McGlory et al., 2019), so day one is the same 2 softgels as every other day.
  • Timing: no time of day is established as best (Lawson and Hughes, 1988; Alijani et al., 2025). Taking with a meal may improve tolerability (NIH ODS, 2025; Alijani et al., 2025), so whichever meal suits your day fits the directions.

What PeakFuel says about EPA and DHA, in full

  • 480 mg EPA and 320 mg DHA per 2-softgel serving. PeakFuel Omega-3 provides EPA and DHA, which are omega-3 fatty acids (NIH ODS, 2025; Calder, 2018; Swanson et al., 2012), alongside 900 mg total omega-3s which include EPA, DHA and other omega-3 fatty acids and 1,200 mg fish oil concentrate in each serving.
  • Where they go. Omega-3 fatty acids are incorporated into cell membranes throughout the body (Calder, 2018; Swanson et al., 2012).
  • What they can change. EPA and DHA can alter membrane composition and influence cell signalling (Calder, 2018; Swanson et al., 2012).

Plasma first, then cell membranes

EPA and DHA from a supplement → EPA and DHA in blood plasma → EPA and DHA in cell membranes → possible shifts in membrane makeup and cell signalling

Swap part of the flour in a bread dough for a different kind and you still have dough. Its makeup can shift, though. In your body, the dough is the cell membrane, and the chain above ends with that kind of shift.

All supplement forms increase plasma EPA and DHA levels, according to the National Institutes of Health, Office of Dietary Supplements (2025) and absorption research by Dyerberg et al. (2010), Lawson and Hughes (1988) and Alijani et al. (2025), a finding that covers the second step of the chain and no further.

What people ask about fish oil before a first order

"I keep seeing mixed headlines about fish oil. What is PeakFuel claiming, then?"

PeakFuel makes no health-outcome claims. Each finding on this page names its sources where it is cited.

"What if the oil has gone rancid?"

Smell or taste cannot reliably measure quality (Hands et al., 2024), so the record to check is the certificate of analysis for the lot number on your bottle, which shows what that batch was tested for. If the softgels have a strong, sharp or unusual smell or taste, stop use and contact us.

"Is it okay with my blood thinner?"

Omega-3 dietary supplements have the potential to interact with warfarin and similar anticoagulants (NIH ODS, 2025; Javaid et al., 2024). Ask the clinician who manages your anticoagulant whether to add an omega-3.
